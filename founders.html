Who we are

Two founders, one specialty

The firm is new. The work isn't. Between us we've spent decades shipping software — and the last few years doing the specific thing this company exists for: getting AI features to a quality you'd put in front of a customer, an auditor, or your own on-call rotation.

01

Why we started this

We kept seeing the same thing from two different seats: AI that demoed well and then wobbled in production, spend that climbed with no one able to explain it, and teams who'd been sold a strategy when what they actually needed was a bar. AI Consultants exists to do that middle part — the unglamorous work between a promising pilot and something an on-call engineer can live with.

Where we actually are. This is a new firm, and you would be among our first clients. What that buys you: senior people doing the work themselves, a founding-client rate, and more of our direct attention than we'll be able to offer once we're booked. What it doesn't buy you yet is a wall of logos — the worked examples are exactly how we'd run an engagement, built end to end, but they're demonstrations, not past results. We'd rather you know that going in.

The founders

Eric Salerno

Co-founder

Eric is a technical product manager who builds — he designs the architecture and ships the system, not just the backlog. Close to 30 years in software, starting as a full-stack developer and moving through program management at Microsoft and Amazon to director-level roles in health tech.

Most recently, at the customer-data platform Amperity, he designed and built an AI-assisted integration system: a human-in-the-loop discovery pipeline feeding a containerized execution engine. It cut connector development from about four weeks to about two days, with first-pass output roughly 85% compatible with Amperity's existing production integration library. Twenty-four connectors reached production; a companion intake-automation step took a request from raw ask to dev-ready spec in about an hour, down from a week.

Before that: Senior Director of Technical Program Management at Press Ganey, reporting to the CTO, where a feasibility analysis he led produced a FedRAMP no-go recommendation that spared the company a costly multi-year program. Senior Program Manager at Microsoft — he led the initial API design for the PlayReady DRM platform and shipped it for Windows 8 and Silverlight, then ran the security-hardening program for Xbox One offline licensing. Senior TPM at Amazon on the Amazon Coins team, building fraud-detection and customer-segmentation models. Director of TPM at Accolade, leading a multi-year CRM migration and integrating two acquisitions into the core platform.

How we work

It's just the two of us today. Every engagement is delivered by a founder, not handed to a junior team. Where a pillar needs hands-on engineering beyond what two people can cover — a large platform migration especially — we're candid about that up front and scope it accordingly (see the team model). We start with a discovery conversation, not a pitch (Problem space), and we leave when your team can run it without us (Engagement model).

Our point of view on where AI does and doesn't earn its keep is on Principles. The short version: judicious, not maximal, and measured before and after.
