Working definition · draft · September 2026

A consultancy for the judicious use of AI in software

We help software organizations use AI where it actually earns its keep — and stop pretending it's a strategy everywhere else. This site is the two of us working out, in public-but-not-really-public, what that company is before we take it in front of a client.

01

Why now

AI tooling crossed from novelty to genuinely production-capable in the last couple of years. Most organizations' adoption hasn't caught up with that — and the gap between what's possible and what's actually happening, disciplined, is the addressable market.

In the businesses we talk to, that gap shows up as one of a small number of patterns: no one's tried anything yet and doesn't know where to start; teams have tried plenty and none of it is trustworthy enough to depend on; spend is climbing with no one able to say whether it's buying anything; three different AI tools are in use with three different bills and no plan; or the SDLC itself hasn't been touched by any of it beyond autocomplete in an editor. We built the five pillars on Services around those patterns because we keep seeing them, not because they made a tidy list.

02

Five ways we help

Each is its own engagement with its own shape. Full detail, including what we explicitly don't do, is on Services.

Pillar 01 AI orientation & opportunity mapping You've heard AI could help. We find the specific, valuable places it actually would — ranked, not hand-waved. Pillar 02 From playground to production Your teams are already experimenting. We turn that into tools you'd trust in front of a customer. Pillar 03 AI cost efficiency Spend is climbing faster than anyone can explain. We find out why, and put a ceiling on it. Pillar 04 Platform consolidation Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, and no plan. We do the hands-on work of standardizing on one, without losing what works. Pillar 05 AI in the SDLC We integrate AI across define, build, test, release, and maintain — the whole cycle, not just the editor.
03

How we work, in brief

We don't sell a pillar before we understand the business. Every engagement starts with a discovery conversation against a fixed set of questions (see Problem space & discovery), followed by a short assessment and a written roadmap tied to a measurable outcome. From there it's either advisory work, hands-on build work, or both, depending on what the roadmap actually calls for — and it ends with enablement, not a dependency on us.
