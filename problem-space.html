How we find fit

Problem space & discovery

Before any pillar gets pitched, we run the same conversation across six areas. It's how we understand a business fast, and it's how we — and they — find out whether we're actually a fit before anyone signs anything.

01

The discovery conversation

Six groups of questions. Not a script — a checklist to make sure we've actually understood the business before we recommend anything.

Current state — where are you today?

  • What does your product/engineering organization look like today — team sizes, roles, delivery cadence?
  • What's the last thing you shipped, and how long did it take from idea to production?
  • Where does work get stuck, historically — definition, build, test, release, or maintenance?

Value delivery — what does "accelerating value to customers" mean for you, specifically?

  • What's the metric that matters — time to market, feature throughput, defect rate, customer-reported issues, something else?
  • What's the cost of a slow cycle to your business, concretely?
  • Who's feeling the delay — external customers, internal stakeholders, both?

AI maturity — where are you between "heard of it" and "running it in production"?

  • Is anyone in the org using AI tools today? Sanctioned, or not?
  • Has anything shipped to production that's AI-assisted or AI-driven?
  • Is there an existing policy, guideline, or governance process for AI use?

Cost — what do you actually know about what you're spending, and on what?

  • What are you currently spending on AI tools and APIs, and does anyone own that number?
  • Do you know spend per team, per feature, per outcome — or just a total?
  • Has cost ever blocked or slowed an AI initiative?

Platform sprawl — how many AI platforms are actually in use?

  • How many distinct AI tools or platforms are in active use across the org, officially and unofficially?
  • Has anyone tried to consolidate before? What happened?
  • Is there a mandate — or an appetite — for a single "golden provider"?

SDLC — where in define → build → test → release → maintain is the biggest drag?

  • Walk us through your SDLC stage by stage — where's the friction?
  • What tooling exists today at each stage, AI or not?
  • What would "faster, without getting worse" actually look like to you?
02

Fit rubric

A rough read on whether we can actually help — used internally after a discovery conversation, not shared verbatim with the client.

SignalGreen — good fitYellow — workableRed — not yet
AI maturity Some real usage already, wants direction Zero usage, genuinely curious Wants a magic fix with no internal ownership
Decision authority Talking to someone who can act on findings A champion exists, needs to build the case upward No one in the room can approve next steps
Engineering culture Existing quality/review discipline to extend Discipline exists but inconsistent No delivery discipline to build on — not an AI problem yet
Budget reality Already spending, wants it to work harder Budget approved in principle, not yet allocated No budget, exploring "what would it cost"
