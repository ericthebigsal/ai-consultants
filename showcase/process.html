Process

The model does the mechanical work. Your team makes the calls.

Building a design system from an existing product is mostly inventory: cataloguing every screen, every color, every one-off. That work is where the manual version stalls — usually around week eight, at the documentation step, when nobody has the time. AI does that ~70%. The team's hours go to the 30% that needs judgment, and the timeline compresses from two-plus months to about three weeks.

~3 weeks to v1 then ongoing governance 1 named owner on your side

Five stages

Audit → Extract → Systematize → Document → Govern

Each stage has an AI lane and a human lane. The red dot marks a decision only your team should make.

Stage
AI does
Your team decides
Auditweek 1
Crawls the live product, captures every screen and state, extracts colors, type, spacing and components into a raw inventory, and clusters the near-duplicates.
What's in scope, and which screens actually matter versus the ones nobody uses.
Extractweek 1–2
Proposes token ramps and names from where each value is used, fits the type sizes to a scale, and drafts component specs from the most common real instances.
Which patterns are real and which are accidents; the names, in your team's language, not the model's.
Systematizeweek 2–3
Generates the missing states, checks every color pair for contrast, outputs the token files in the formats your stack needs, and wires the build.
Interaction feel, the hard trade-offs, and what earns being a component versus staying a one-off.
Documentweek 3
Drafts the reference pages from the specs, writes first-pass usage guidance, and keeps the docs in sync with the token files as they change.
The “why” behind each rule, and the examples that actually teach it.
Governongoing
Runs a weekly drift check against production, groups the findings, and opens each one as a proposed fix to review.
What counts as drift worth fixing versus a documented exception; approval on every addition.
Why this order. Audit before Extract because you can't systematize patterns you haven't seen. Document before Govern because there's nothing to enforce until it's written down. The stages don't move until the human lane is done — the model never advances the work on its own.

At the end

What you get, and what stays yours

You get

  • Token files in your stack's formats
  • Component specs with every state
  • The reference site (these pages)
  • The weekly drift check wired into your CI
  • A half-day session so your team runs it without us

Stays yours

  • Every naming and scoping decision
  • The brand intent behind the system
  • Accessibility sign-off
  • The call on what's a real pattern
  • The system itself — we hand it over and leave
The honest limit. AI is fast at cataloguing and drafting and terrible at taste. If your product's design is genuinely good and just undocumented, this is three weeks well spent. If the design itself is the problem, that's a different engagement, and we'll say so.
