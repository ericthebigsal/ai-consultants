AI vendor register · Q3

Seven AI vendors, three doing the same job, and no plan to leave any of them.

Northwind spends about $340k a year across seven AI vendors. Three of them — picked by different teams — overlap almost entirely. This is the register, the head-to-head that picks the one to keep, and what it would take to exit each of the others.

04 · Keeping it current

The register doesn't go stale

Maintain Watches the expense feed and SSO app list for new AI vendors, tracks the renewal calendar, and flags vendor pricing or terms changes from public sources before a renewal date. the negotiation, and the decision on any new vendor request.
  • New-vendor watch. A new AI charge or app triggers a register entry and a one-line "why not an existing vendor?" question to the requesting team.
  • Renewal calendar. Every contract's renewal and notice-period date, with a reminder 60 days out and the current usage and cost attached.
  • Terms watch. Public changes to a vendor's pricing, model deprecations, or data-use policy are flagged against the affected register lines.
What we hand over. The register wired to the expense and SSO feeds, the scorecard method and data, the migration plans for B and C, the renewal calendar, and a working session so Northwind's procurement and platform leads run it together.
