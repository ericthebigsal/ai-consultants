What an engagement produces

Four illustrative engagements, built end to end for fictional clients. They stand in for the deliverables a real engagement would hand over — a systematized design foundation, an AI-governance and vendor picture, and a full capability POC with a business case and a statement of work. Every page is labelled as a sample; nothing here is a real client.

01

Northwind Analytics

A fictional data-analytics SaaS whose product design accreted across several teams. The engagement covers using AI to systematize the design layer and get the AI-operations picture under control — each page threads a visible “AI pass / human decision” margin note. Its own identity: petrol, chalk, Bricolage Grotesque.

Design Design system Token tables, component specs, and a weekly drift check — rebuilt from an audit of 47 screens. Design Brand guidelines Wordmark rules, colour meaning, and four voice principles with worked do/avoid rewrites. Design Process The five-stage AI-augmented design process as an AI-lane / human-lane swimlane. Operations AI governance A register of where AI touches sensitive data, an enforceable control set, and a framework crosswalk. Operations AI vendors A vendor register with keep/consolidate/exit calls, a workload-based scorecard, and exit readiness.
02

Chateau Salerno

A fictional winery-commerce SaaS — a marketing platform for small wineries — that wants to add AI capabilities and modernize. A full pitch package: a business case and a statement of work in the docs catalog, plus four clickable capability prototypes built around a sample winery, Kestrel Ridge Vineyards. Its own identity: bordeaux, parchment, Newsreader.

Overview The prototype The four capabilities and how to read them — what's real interface vs. pre-generated output. Capability 01 NL segments A plain-English audience resolves to a normal, editable segment — filter tree, count, sample. Capability 02 Journey drafting A goal produces a 5-step email + SMS journey with branch logic and copy in the winery's voice. Capability 03 Churn & LTV Club members scored on cancellation risk and predicted value, with the factors behind each score. Capability 04 Tasting notes & copy Structured wine data drafts the tasting note, shop description, and allocation email in one voice.
03

APlusTechBros

A fictional SaaS martech platform that shipped AI into eight features and never checked the bill — ~$182k/month, no attribution, no evals, keys in the frontend. This engagement is remediation: attribute the spend, route each feature to the right model, put everything behind an eval, fix the architecture. A business case and statement of work in the docs catalog, plus four analysis dashboards. Its own identity: electric indigo, Archivo, a before → after ledger throughout.

Overview The prototype The four analyses and the illustrative-figures framing — $182k/mo down to ~$74k, two features cut. Analysis 01 Cost & usage audit The AI bill broken down by feature, model, and cost per outcome, with ~$108k/mo of waste named in dollars. Analysis 02 Model routing Each feature routed to the cheapest tier that passes its eval — a 59% cut before the two feature cuts. Analysis 03 Eval harness Per-feature win rate vs. a deterministic baseline. Six keep, two cut for losing to the baseline. Analysis 04 Prompt & call diet One system prompt 4,240 → 680 tokens with quality held; a six-call panel collapsed to one.
04

Fieldwork

A fictional company building a survey platform, where a medium feature takes about four weeks from written requirement to production. This engagement uses Claude Code across the SDLC to roughly halve that — keeping every one of the six review gates. A business case and statement of work in the docs catalog, plus four demos. Its own identity: spruce green, Instrument Sans, an ochre “human gate” marker throughout.

Overview The prototype The four parts and the illustrative-figures framing — ~4 weeks down to ~1.7, same six gates. Part 01 The cycle Each SDLC stage timed before and after Claude Code, with the drafting/deciding split. Part 02 A feature Response quotas per segment, from a one-paragraph requirement to a merged PR — every artifact, every gate. Part 03 The setup The CLAUDE.md, skills, subagents, MCP servers, hooks, and slash commands configured for the repo. Part 04 The guardrails The six gates, the TDD rule, the CI checks, and what Claude Code is not allowed to touch.

How to read these

Northwind Analytics, Chateau Salerno, APlusTechBros, Fieldwork, and Kestrel Ridge Vineyards are invented, and so are all the figures, vendors, and data. Each engagement gets its own visual identity on purpose — the point of the design work is a client-specific system, not a house style.

The interfaces and flows are representative of what a build would produce. Where a page shows model output — a drafted journey, a generated tasting note — it is pre-generated for a fixed set of inputs and marked as such. Nothing on these pages calls a model or stores anything.
