Capability 02

A goal in. A journey out.

Kestrel Ridge’s marketer describes the outcome. The platform drafts the multi-step email and SMS journey — timing, branch logic, and first-draft copy in the winery’s voice — on the same canvas they already edit.

The goal

Audience: lapsed Aviary — no order 9+ mo — 212 members Window: 3 weeks Channels: email + SMS

Drafted journey Pre-generated

Step 1 Email Day 0 — all 212

Subject: The east ridge came in late this year

Opens on the vineyard and the vintage — the cool fall, the late pick on the ridge’s east shoulder. Names the fall release date. One soft line at the end about holding their Aviary spot. No discount.

Why: lead with place, not the ask. A lapsed member who left over money won’t come back for a coupon; one who drifted will come back for the wine.

Step 2 SMS Day 4 — only if Step 1 unopened

“Kestrel Ridge: fall release opens the 15th. Your Aviary spot is held through the 12th — reply YES to keep it.”

Why: SMS reaches the members email didn’t. A real, near deadline — the 12th — not manufactured urgency.

Step 3 Email Day 8 — all still lapsed

Subject: What was in the spring shipment

Shows the specific wines they missed at their tier — the 2022 Estate Pinot, the 2023 Rosé that sold out — with a short tasting-room note on each. Ends with the fall allocation link.

Why: a concrete, tier-specific picture of what was missed lands harder than “we miss you.”

Step 4 Email Day 14 — branch: opened Step 3

Subject: Hold your fall allocation

One-click reactivate. Falconer-tier members also see that Block 7 access comes back with their membership. Short, direct — this is the conversion step.

Why: only sent to the engaged branch, so it can be a plain ask instead of another soft touch.

Step 5 SMS Day 19 — branch: no reactivation

“Last call — fall allocations lock tomorrow. [link]”

Why: a single final touch. After this, the member exits the journey to the quarterly re-engagement list — not more sends.

On the canvas

Day 0 Step 1 · Email ──────────────┐ │ unopened Day 4 Step 2 · SMS ◀────────────────┘ │ Day 8 Step 3 · Email ─── opened ──▶ Day 14 Step 4 · Email ─── reactivated ──▶ done │ not opened / not reactivated Day 19 Step 5 · SMS ─────────────────────────────────────▶ quarterly list
Every step is a normal journey step. The model set the structure, the timing, and the first-draft copy; the marketer edits each step in place and hits publish. Nothing about the canvas or the step types is new.

In a build

  • Copy is drafted from the winery’s own past sends and product data — its voice, not a generic template. The spring-shipment step pulls the actual wines from that shipment.
  • Branch logic uses engagement events the platform already tracks — opened, clicked, reactivated — so the branches are testable, not black-box.
  • Nothing sends until the marketer approves the journey. The draft is a starting point, not an auto-send.
