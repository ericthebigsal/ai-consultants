The model explains the score in the platform’s own events — visits, allocation choices, opens — not a number with no handle on it. The strongest signal here is the shift to “hold,” which usually comes before a cancel.

In a build

  • The score is retrained weekly on the winery’s own history — its members, its seasonality, its shipping patterns.
  • “Hold” and “skip” allocation events are the strongest early signal; they show up weeks before a cancellation.
  • A member drops off the at-risk list automatically once they re-engage, so the marketer isn’t chasing stale flags.
At-risk members are just a segment. Anything that consumes a segment — the segment engine, journeys, exports — can use them without anything new being built.
