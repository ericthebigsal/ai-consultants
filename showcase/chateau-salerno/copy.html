Tasting note

The east shoulder of the ridge ripens about a week behind the rest of the vineyard, and in a cool year like 2022 that patience shows. This is the darker, quieter Block 7 — more structure than fruit, tight now, better in three or four years. Save it for a cold night and something braised.

Shop description

Block 7 sits on the 1998 planting on the ridge’s east shoulder, the last fruit we bring in most years. 2022 was a long, cool vintage — we picked on September 28, later than we like, and the wine came in with real spine. Fourteen months in French oak, a third of it new, then bottled unfined. Just 180 cases. It’s built to be put away; if you open one now, give it an hour in a decanter and plan on a second bottle in 2027.
