The three that matter

Content assistant, tag & classification, and the insights panel are 70% of the bill — and all three are the easy fixes.

Content assistant — no cache

The product-description generator re-runs on every catalogue page load. The same 40,000 products get regenerated dozens of times a day with no change to their inputs. A cache keyed on the product fields cuts an estimated 60% of the calls.

Calls / month8.2M→~3.3M−60%

Tag & classification — wrong tier

14 million events a month get a category label from the frontier model. This is a bounded classification task with ~20 labels — a small fine-tuned classifier does it at a fraction of the cost and slightly higher accuracy.

Cost / month$44k→~$3.5k−92%

AI Insights panel — 6 calls per view

Each dashboard panel load makes six sequential model calls: fetch context, summarize, detect anomalies, rank, write the headline, write the body. One call with a structured output schema returns all of it.

Calls per panel view6→1−83%

What the audit produces

  • A per-feature cost model, wired to the provider's usage export, that updates without anyone re-running a spreadsheet.
  • A cost-per-outcome for each feature — per kept draft, per deflection, per send — so the next question ("is it worth it?") has a number.
  • A ranked list of fixes with the dollar impact of each, which becomes the phase order in the statement of work.
Attribution is the whole first phase. Every later decision — route this feature, cut that one — needs a per-feature number behind it, and today there isn't one.
